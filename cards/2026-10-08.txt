Track: COBIT 2019

COBIT 2019: 5 domains, 40 objectives

THE IDEA
<cite index="1-1,6-1">COBIT 2019 contains 40 governance and management objectives.</cite> <cite index="6-2,24-12">Five belong to the EDM governance domain and the remaining thirty five are spread across the APO, BAI, DSS and MEA management domains.</cite> <cite index="11-2,20-3">The governance and management objectives in COBIT 2019 are grouped into 5 domains.</cite> EDM is for the board. APO plans and aligns. BAI builds solutions. DSS runs operations. MEA monitors compliance and performance.

KEY TERMS
EDM (Evaluate, Direct, Monitor) · APO (Align, Plan, Organize) · BAI (Build, Acquire, Implement) · DSS (Deliver, Service, Support) · MEA (Monitor, Evaluate, Assess)

WHY IT MATTERS
This is the structure Mas Yoga wants you to adapt for network and cybersecurity. The five-domain logic separates governance from management, a distinction that will shape accountability and decision rights when autonomous networks push operational changes into production without waiting for human approval.

THINK ABOUT
When a network controller reconfigures a link autonomously, which COBIT domain owns the approval threshold policy, and which domain records evidence that the policy was followed?
