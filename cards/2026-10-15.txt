CGEIT Domain 4: Risk Optimization

THE IDEA
Risk Optimization ensures an IT risk management framework exists to identify, analyze, mitigate, manage, monitor and communicate IT-related business risk and aligns it with enterprise risk management. The framework balances protection against opportunity, addresses legal and regulatory compliance, and establishes key risk indicators and escalation protocols.

KEY TERMS
IT risk management framework · Enterprise risk alignment · Risk appetite and tolerance · Key risk indicators · Risk escalation protocols

WHY IT MATTERS
For autonomous networks, Risk Optimization defines who approves automated changes, sets guardrails for AI actions, and determines when human oversight is mandatory. These controls must span IT, network and cybersecurity in your unified framework.

THINK ABOUT
At what autonomy level should your governance framework require human approval before a network agent executes a configuration change?
