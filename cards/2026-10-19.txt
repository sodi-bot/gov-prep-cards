eTOM Operations: Fulfilment, Assurance and Billing

THE IDEA
The Operations area of eTOM contains the core customer-facing processes organized vertically as Fulfilment, Assurance and Billing, and horizontally across functional domains like Customer Relationship Management, Service Management and Operations, and Resource Management and Operations. These Level 1 and Level 2 processes map network lifecycle activities to a standard business process structure, enabling end-to-end flow-through from customer order to resource provisioning and ongoing service quality.

KEY TERMS
Fulfilment Assurance Billing · Operations Level 2 · Service and Resource Management · Horizontal and Vertical groupings

WHY IT MATTERS
Understanding where network operations processes sit in eTOM lets you map COBIT IT governance to the network domain and identify decision rights and approval points for autonomous network changes across the unified framework.

THINK ABOUT
Which eTOM Level 2 processes in Resource Management and Operations will autonomous network actions touch, and where will you insert approval gates?
