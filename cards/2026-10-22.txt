ISO 27011: Telecom-Specific Security Controls

THE IDEA
ISO/IEC 27011 adapts ISO 27002 controls for telecommunications operators. Published March 2024, it adds telecom-specific guidance on interconnect security, DoS mitigation, signaling protection, and lawful intercept. It supplements ISO 27001 certification and aligns with ITU-T X.1051. In Indonesia, strategic and high-risk systems must implement ISO 27001 standards.

KEY TERMS
ISO/IEC 27011:2024 · Telecom-specific controls · ITU-T X.1051 alignment · Indonesian BSSN requirements

WHY IT MATTERS
Your unified framework can use ISO 27011 as the telecom sector extension of ISO 27001, bridging generic IT security controls with network-specific threats like signaling attacks and ensuring alignment with Indonesian strategic-system mandates.

THINK ABOUT
Which network assets in your scope would trigger strategic or high-risk classification under Indonesian rules, requiring ISO 27001 controls?
