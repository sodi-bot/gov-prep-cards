Crosswalks: Mapping One Framework to Another

THE IDEA
A crosswalk is a structured mapping that connects requirements across multiple frameworks, identifying where controls are equivalent, dependent or distinct. It lets you implement a control once and reuse the evidence across COBIT, NIST CSF, ISO 27001 and TM Forum, reducing duplication, exposing gaps and strengthening audit traceability.

KEY TERMS
Crosswalk mapping · Control equivalence · Multi-framework compliance · Mapping relationship strength

WHY IT MATTERS
Your unified framework needs explicit mappings between COBIT objectives, TM Forum eTOM processes, NIST CSF subcategories and ISO 27001 controls. Crosswalks demonstrate that one network governance control satisfies multiple requirements without duplicate effort.

THINK ABOUT
Which network governance controls in your organisation are currently implemented separately for IT audit, operations and security, and could be unified?
