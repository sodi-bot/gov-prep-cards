Capability and Maturity Levels for Network Governance

THE IDEA
COBIT 2019 uses six capability levels (0 to 5) per process and aggregates them into maturity scores for focus areas. TM Forum defines six autonomous network levels (L0 to L5) to measure operational autonomy from manual to fully self-managing. Both scales provide measurable baselines, track governance improvement, and anchor decision rights and assurance requirements as autonomy increases.

KEY TERMS
Capability levels 0-5 · Maturity assessment · TM Forum L0-L5 · Process versus focus area

WHY IT MATTERS
Your unified framework needs a common measurement language across IT, network, and cybersecurity. Capability and maturity levels let you baseline current state, set improvement targets, define governance thresholds for automated network changes, and report progress to leadership and auditors.

THINK ABOUT
Which network operational processes sit at L2 or below today, and what governance guardrails must you define before advancing them to L3 conditional autonomy?
