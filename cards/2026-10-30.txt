Your First 90 Days as Manager Network Governance

THE IDEA
The first 90 days establish credibility through listening, not enforcement. Understand the current state through stakeholder conversations, document existing governance gaps without demanding quick fixes, and align your mandate with the business driver that created the role. Assess capability and political resistance before designing the unified framework.

KEY TERMS
30-60-90 day plan · Stakeholder mapping · Current state assessment · Quick wins versus structure · Change resistance patterns

WHY IT MATTERS
Your manager expects a unified COBIT-TM Forum framework spanning IT, network and cybersecurity. Success depends on understanding existing processes, politics and capability gaps before proposing governance that will meet resistance. Listen first.

THINK ABOUT
Who will oppose the unified framework most strongly, and what existing process or authority will they defend?
