Track: Autonomous Networks

Who Decides When the Network Decides?

THE IDEA
As autonomous networks progress toward L4 and L5, accountability for AI-driven decisions must remain with humans, not the model. Guardrails define approval thresholds by criticality, scope and reversibility. NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, TM Forum and ETSI ZSM all emphasize human oversight, risk-based decision rights and auditable evidence that a person owns the consequences.

KEY TERMS
Decision rights · Human-in-the-loop · Approval thresholds · Accountability framework · Guardrails

WHY IT MATTERS
Your unified framework must define who owns autonomous decisions, what requires human approval and how evidence proves accountability. TM Forum, NIST and ISO all treat this as a control, not a prompt.

THINK ABOUT
Which network changes will your framework allow autonomously, which need approval, and who signs the policy defining the threshold?
