AI Governance Standards for Networks: NIST and ISO

THE IDEA
NIST AI Risk Management Framework and ISO/IEC 42001 are the two leading horizontal AI governance standards. NIST AI RMF organizes risk work into four functions: Govern, Map, Measure and Manage. ISO 42001 is the first certifiable management system standard for AI. Both apply to autonomous network deployments and can be mapped into a unified IT, network and cybersecurity governance framework.

KEY TERMS
NIST AI RMF · ISO/IEC 42001 · Four functions · Certifiable AIMS

WHY IT MATTERS
TM Forum is launching a Trustworthy AI Governance Certification for operators scaling autonomous networks. NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 are both referenced in telecom autonomous-network governance work, and both integrate with COBIT and ISO 27001.

THINK ABOUT
Which of the four NIST AI RMF functions do you expect to meet the most resistance when applied to network closed loops, and why?
